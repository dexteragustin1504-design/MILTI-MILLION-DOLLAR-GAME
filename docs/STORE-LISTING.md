**Full description:**

One spark against the swarm.

Pilot a tiny ship through a glowing arena. Your weapons target enemies automatically, leaving you free to weave through danger, gather energy, and time your next dash.

Survive each wave to choose an upgrade. Split your fire, accelerate your arsenal, repair your hull, or pull energy from farther away. Combine upgrades to build a run worth remembering.

- Drag to move and tap to dash
- Escalating enemy waves and upgrade choices
- Procedural neon visuals and optional sound
- Offline play with best scores saved on your device

No account required. Current runs restart if the app is closed; completed best scores remain until app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.
